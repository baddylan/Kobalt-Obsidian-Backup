##### Winter (2022 IRL)
###### DECEMBER:
[[Iridic]] is dropped off at a school by a space marine working on a privateer vessel hunting pirates and slavers, the privateer is hoping St. Mary's will offer a bounty. Iridic is dropped off in a stasis pod after a nurse exchanges some out of medical spec cybernetics instead. The privateer reported that 'she' was the only survivor of a slave vessel that incinerated all other cargo, that 'her' pod malfunctioned and spaired 'her' but that she is unfit to become a marine. The marine says that they let 'her' out and shortly after she freaked out and injured several crew and they had to repack 'her' forcefully.
Upon arrival Iridic is malnourished, suffering from a drug based memory cleansing routine, has a broken tentacle and several broken fingers as well as a concussion, the pod only had enough chemicals for a liminal dose that has made Iridic's memory of events sketchy at best the second time around. The pod was marked Iridian Xenopanther, apparently Iridic was able to read the pod and named themself Iridic Xenopanther.
Iridic is put on campus arrest for their own safety.
###### JANUARY:
It is noted that Iridic is not putting on weight as expected.
Iridic comes under attack by a puma that infiltrated the school basement and ambushed them in an act of sexual violence before being killed by a teacher with his bare hands. In addition to the normal trauma Iridic suffered a fractured skull, severe concussion, and several more broken fingers from their struggle. School security launched an investigation.
###### FEBRUARY:
Iridic still has not put on weight, they are taken to the hospital and it is noted that they are an obligate carnivore that becomes incapable of absorbing nutrients for several hours after ingesting wheat and some other related grains. They are put on a strict carnivorous diet.
###### MARCH:
Iridic has put on some weight and started to develop breasts, school cafeteria staff report that they are not eating all of their meals.
Records of frequent injuries to Iridic and other students are recorded as evidence of frequent fights, teachers are put on watch.
###### APRIL:
Iridic is put on a food watch with nurse checkups and room visits if meals are missed.
Iridic's dorm room is sound proofed to help with sleep.
###### JULY:
Iridic has put on significant muscle mass well outside of expected norms appears to religiously go to gym morning and evening. (This continues to date)
Iridic requests binders from the school clothier and nurses take note of evidence of tight binding (This continues until January 2024 IRL).
###### AUGUST:
Like all students Iridic is put on birth control to avoid too many teenage pregnancies at the school as is standard practice for students of all sexes and genders.
###### November:
Iridic begins to complain about their birthcontrol, medical examination reveals that they are not ovulating or producing a uterine lining in spite of external pheromones and tests suggesting otherwise. Iridic is allowed to go off birth control and nurses note that there must be some sort of developmental damage from all that the kid has been through.
###### December: 
Iridic is allowed to join the rest of the school on retreats for the first time in return for a promise of good behavior.
###### (2023 IRL)
Evidence of fights continues through this entire year.
Counsellors begin to report that Iridic is doing well in the classes they accept of their own accord and acts rebelliously in classes they see as unimportant.
Behavior improves once they are allowed to select their own classes, Iridic excels in STEM and crafting courses and rebels at language and history courses, apparent mental health improves
Tight binding gets worse as Iridic continues to grow, muscle mass grows, some nurses and teachers record that Iridic is attempting to work out enough to stay flat.
No serious incidents occur.
###### DECEMBER:
During winter break Iridic approaches the headmaster for magical intervention for their chest.
###### (2024 IRL)
The number of fights Iridic gets in continues to reduce, but the apparent altercations seem to repeat with several of the same students over apparent bullying and retaliation, it is unclear in all cases who starts the altercation, Iridic clearly holds grudges and goads students into arguments and fights.
###### January:
Iridic undergoes magical assistance for breast reduction with the aid of the headmaster, stops tight binding, and is taken off of campus arrest.
###### APRIL:
During spring break Iridic is recorded as getting into several severe physical altercations in a short time frame and requires assistance from one Mrs. Veritas before the cruise returns.
