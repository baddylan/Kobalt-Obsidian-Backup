It started not long after [[Kieran]] met [[Books]], who soon ended up the first member of the ship's crew. He later ended up with [[Dys]] joining the crew as the Chief Engineer. [[Iridic]] ended up part of the crew at some point as the Ship's Mechanic, followed by [[Cherna]] as the Ship's Counselor. [[Dexter]] soon joined as the Environmental Technician. Most recently, [[Kobalt]] joined the crew as Chief Medical Officer, in charge of the Medical Bay.

The Castaway King Crew includes [[Kieran]], [[Books]], [[Dys]], [[Iridic]], [[Cherna]], [[Dexter]], and [[Kobalt]].
