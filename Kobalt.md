[[Kobalt]] Amber is a nine tailed Kitsune, originally from one of the versions of Earth. He appears to only be 29 years old, but in reality, he's 5,738 years old. He's a doctor, and has served aboard many starships as the Chief Medical Officer at this point. He has quite a bit of experience as a pilot and engineer as well, but doesn't use those skills as much anymore. He has spent a couple thousand years working as a doctor, and is confident in his skills and abilities, yet able to know when he needs help if he's not got the needed experience with something. He's currently in a relationship with [[Fate|Fate Everywhere]], as well as a new relationship with [[Badru]].

He's ended up part of the [[Castaway King Crew]], as their Chief Medical Officer, working on keeping track of [[Crew Medical Records]]. He met [[Kieran]] not long after visiting the Drop Zone. Soon after that, he met [[Books]], as well as [[Cherna]], [[Iridic]], [[Dexter]], and [[Dys]].

Most recently he consulted with Dr. [[Mateja]] Moravec about Dys and his injuries after noticing something odd when he got the records from her.
